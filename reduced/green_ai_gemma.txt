=== PITFALLS DISCOVERY ===
Okay, here’s an analysis of potential ontological pitfalls within the Green-AI Ontology, along with suggested solutions, focusing on practical axioms and resolving the issues.

**Pitfall 1: Granularity of Hardware Properties**

* **Problem:** The ontology has many properties describing hardware (CPU, GPU, Memory, Storage, etc.) at a relatively coarse granularity.  For example, `hasCPUCount` and `hasGPUCount` only capture the *number* and not specifics like clock speed, model type, or efficiency.  Similarly, ‘hasMemory’ just states the memory size.
* **Solution:** Introduce more specific classes and properties.
* **Axiom:**
    * `ObjectProperty :hasCPUType extends Class :CPU`
    * `ObjectProperty :hasGPUType extends Class :GPU`
    * Introduce new datatype properties e.g. :`CPUClockSpeed`, :`GPUMemoryBandwidth.`


**Pitfall 2:  Lack of Explicit Energy Efficiency Metrics**

* **Problem:** While `hasEnergyMetrics` and `hasEnergyMetricsAllExperiments` exist, there's no explicit connection between these metrics and hardware characteristics (CPU/GPU model, power consumption). There’s no single measure of energy *efficiency* (FLOPS/Watt).
* **Solution:** Combine energy metrics with hardware specifics.
* **Axiom:**
   * `ObjectProperty :hasEfficiency extends Class :EnergyMeasure;
      Domain: :AIModel;
      Range: :EnergyMeasure` –  This links the AI model to its energy efficiency.
    * `ObjectProperty :FLOPSperWatt extends Class :HardwareSettings` – linked to each hardware setting.


**Pitfall 3:  Limited Representation of Software Dependencies**

* **Problem:**  `hasSoftwareSettings` exists, but doesn't capture dependencies between software packages, libraries, or the operating system.
* **Solution:**  Introduce a module type/structure with dependencies or a relationship describing which software package is required to use which feature.
* **Axiom:** `ObjectProperty :uses extends Class :Module;
   Domain: :SoftwareSettings;
   Range: :Module` 


**Pitfall 4:  Vague Representatio of ‘Training’**

* **Problem:** `trainedOn` just links an AI model to a dataset. It doesn’t capture the training process (optimization algorithm, hyperparameters, duration).
* **Solution:** Add properties to refine the training process.
* **Axiom:** Introduce new properties like `trainingAlgorithm`, `hyperparameters`, `trainingDuration.`

**Pitfall 5: Implicit association between experiments and measurements**

* **Problem:** The ‘All Experiments’ aspects seem to be implicitly linked to the model, rather than linked by an explicit experiment class.
* **Solution:** Introduce an experiment class with associated measurement
* **Axiom:** Introduce a new class `Experiment` and `ExperimentResult` and create the links relevant to this.


**Pitfall 6: Datatypes are inconsistent**
* **Problem:** It is inconsistent in the definitions of the dimension of the role property
* **Solution:** Standardisation of the datatypes, especially the floats
