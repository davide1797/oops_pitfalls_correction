=== PITFALLS DISCOVERY ===
### **Ontological Pitfalls & Resolutions (OntoPFAS)**

#### **1. Inconsistent Property Domains/Ranges**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:affectedBy` and `ontopfas:affects` have mismatched domains/ranges:
  - `affectedBy` (domain: `schema:Place`, range: `obo:ExO_0000022`) vs.
  - `affects` (domain: `obo:ExO_0000022`, range: `schema:Place`).
- **Resolution**: Ensure symmetry and logical consistency by enforcing:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:affectedBy owl:inverseOf ontopfas:affects .
  ```

#### **2. Misaligned Inverse Properties**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:source_of` (domain: `dcat:Dataset`, range: `owl:Thing`) lacks a clear inverse for `dcterms:source` (range: `dcat:Dataset`).
- **Resolution**: Define explicit inverse and enforce domain/range alignment:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:source_of owl:inverseOf dcterms:source .
  dcterms:source rdfs:domain [ rdf:type owl:Thing ] .
  ```

#### **3. Overlapping Restrictions (Long/Short Chain PFAS)**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:LongChainPfas` and `ontopfas:ShortChainPfas` are defined as restrictions but lack formal constraints (e.g., disjointness).
- **Resolution**: Add disjointness axioms to prevent overlap:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:LongChainPfas owl:disjointWith ontopfas:ShortChainPfas .
  ```

#### **4. Ambiguous `ontopfas:PfasMobility` Usage**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:mobility` (domain: `Pfas`, range: `PfasMobility`) and `ontopfas:mobilityInEnvironment` (domain: `PfasMobility`, range: `ENVO_01000408`) create circularity.
- **Resolution**: Clarify hierarchy with:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:PfasMobility rdfs:subClassOf ontopfas:Pfas .
  ontopfas:mobilityInEnvironment rdfs:domain ontopfas:PfasMobility .
  ```

#### **5. Inconsistent `ontopfas:containsMeasurement`**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:containsMeasurement` (domain: `SpatialThing`, range: `sosa:Observation`) conflicts with `ontopfas:measuredIn` (domain: `sosa:Observation`, range: `SpatialThing`).
- **Resolution**: Align definitions and enforce transitivity:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:containsMeasurement owl:inverseOf ontopfas:measuredIn .
  ontopfas:containsMeasurement rdf:type owl:TransitiveProperty .
  ```

#### **6. Misuse of `schema:Organization` as Domain**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:actiivtyInEnvironment` (domain: `schema:Organization`) misrepresents an activity (not an organization).
- **Resolution**: Replace with a dedicated `Activity` class or use `prov:wasGeneratedBy`:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:ActivityInEnvironment rdf:type owl:Class ;
      rdfs:subClassOf schema:Activity .
  ontopfas:actiivtyInEnvironment rdfs:domain ontopfas:ActivityInEnvironment .
  ```

#### **7. `ontopfas:validInPlace` vs. `ontopfas:validInEnvironment`**
- **Pitfall**: Both properties map `Regulation` to `Place`/`ENVO_01000408`, but `ENVO_01000408` (e.g., "Environmental zone") is more specific than `schema:Place`.
- **Resolution**: Restrict `validInPlace` to `schema:Place` and enforce:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:validInPlace rdfs:domain <https://w3id.org/ocqa/regulation#Regulation> ;
      rdfs:range schema:Place .
  ontopfas:validInEnvironment rdfs:domain <https://w3id.org/ocqa/regulation#Regulation> ;
      rdfs:range obo:ENVO_01000408 .
  ```

#### **8. `ontopfas:stimulates`/`stimulatedBy` Domain/Range Mismatch**
- **Pitfall**: `stimulates` (domain: `Pfas`, range: `ExO_0000022`) vs. `stimulatedBy` (domain: `ExO_0000022`, range: `Pfas`) are inverses but lack alignment.
- **Resolution**: Enforce inverse and refine definitions:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:stimulates owl:inverseOf ontopfas:stimulatedBy .
  ```

#### **9. `ontopfas:isEther`/`isHydrophilic` as Boolean DP**
- **Pitfall**: Boolean `DatatypeProperty` values (`xsd:boolean`) may not align with OBO’s `owl:BooleanProperty` best practice.
- **Resolution**: Use `owl:BooleanProperty` (if possible) or add constraints:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:isEther rdfs:range xsd:boolean ;
      owl:onProperty xsd:boolean .
  ```

#### **10. Unclear `ontopfas:FunctionalGroup` Hierarchy**
- **Pitfall**: `FunctionalGroup` is a subclass of `CHEBI_24431` (Chemical entity), but its properties (e.g., `functionalGroupName`) suggest it should be a **role** or **property** rather than a class.
- **Resolution**: Option 1: Reclassify as a `owl:ObjectProperty` (e.g., `ontopfas:hasFunctionalGroup`).
  Option 2: Define as a `owl:Class` with restrictions:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:FunctionalGroup rdfs:subClassOf ontopfas:Pfas .
  ontopfas:functionalGroup owl:equivalentProperty ontopfas:functionalGroupOf .
  ```

#### **11. `ontopfas:PfasPrecursor` vs. `ontopfas:Pfas`**
- **Pitfall**: `PfasPrecursor` is a subclass of `Pfas`, but its definition implies it should be a **disjoint** subclass (precursors ≠ PFAS).
- **Resolution**: Add disjointness:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:PfasPrecursor owl:disjointWith ontopfas:Pfas .
  ```

#### **12. `ontopfas:UltraShortChainPfas` Overlap with `ShortChainPfas`**
- **Pitfall**: `UltraShortChainPfas` (≤3 carbons) is a subclass of `ShortChainPfas` (≤7 for PFCA/≤5 for PFSA), but no formal restriction prevents PFSA from being ultra-short.
- **Resolution**: Add a restriction to enforce PFCA-only:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:UltraShortChainPfas rdfs:subClassOf ontopfas:Pfca .
  ```

#### **13. `ontopfas:VulnerablePerson` Lack of Formal Definition**
- **Pitfall**: No explicit criteria for what makes a `Person` vulnerable (e.g., age, health condition).
- **Resolution**: Add a restriction or property:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:VulnerablePerson rdfs:subClassOf [ a owl:Restriction ;
      owl:onProperty ontopfas:hasHandicap ;
      owl:hasValue true ] .
  ```

#### **14. `ontopfas:mapsLink` as `DatatypeProperty`**
- **Pitfall**: `mapsLink` (range: `xsd:string`) is better suited as an `AnnotationProperty` (metadata) rather than a formal `DatatypeProperty`.
- **Resolution**: Reclassify:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:mapsLink rdf:type owl:AnnotationProperty .
  ```

#### **15. `ontopfas:sourceUrl`/`sourceText` Redundancy**
- **Pitfall**: Both are `DatatypeProperty` for `schema:Organization` but could be merged into a single `owl:ObjectProperty` (e.g., `ontopfas:hasSourceReference`).
- **Resolution**: Define a unified property:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:hasSourceReference rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
      rdfs:subPropertyOf dcterms:source ;
      rdfs:domain schema:Organization ;
      rdfs:range [ a owl:Thing ; rdfs:label "Source reference" ] .
  ```
