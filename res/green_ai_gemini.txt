### Analysis and Solutions:

**P04: Creating unconnected ontology elements**
*   **Affected:** `irao:Software`
*   **Analysis:** The class `irao:Software` is declared (`irao:Software rdf:type owl:Class ; rdfs:label "Software"@en .`) but is not connected to any other classes in the ontology through `rdfs:subClassOf`, `rdfs:domain`, or `rdfs:range`. This makes it an isolated element.
*   **Proposed Axiom:** A logical connection in the context of the Green AI Ontology would be to state that `gao:Module` (which is a component of `gao:SoftwareSettings`) is a type of `irao:Software`.

    ```turtle
    gao:Module rdfs:subClassOf irao:Software .
    ```

**P08: Missing annotations**
*   **Affected:** `vivo:Project`, `foaf:Person`
*   **Analysis:** These classes, while imported or referenced, lack an `rdfs:label` in the provided ontology snippet, which is a common annotation pitfall.
*   **Proposed Axioms:** Add an `rdfs:label` for each.

    ```turtle
    vivo:Project rdfs:label "Project"@en .
    foaf:Person rdfs:label "Person"@en .
    ```

**P10: Missing disjointness**
*   **Analysis:** This pitfall indicates that certain classes that are conceptually distinct are not declared as disjoint, which can lead to inconsistencies or undesired inferences.
*   **Proposed Axioms:**

    1.  `gao:CPU` and `gao:GPU` are mutually exclusive types of hardware.
        ```turtle
        gao:CPU owl:disjointWith gao:GPU .
        ```
    2.  `gao:HardwareSettings` and `gao:SoftwareSettings` represent distinct categories of configuration.
        ```turtle
        gao:HardwareSettings owl:disjointWith gao:SoftwareSettings .
        ```
    3.  `gao:AIModel` and `irao:Dataset` are distinct entities: one is a computational model, the other is data.
        ```turtle
        gao:AIModel owl:disjointWith irao:Dataset .
        ```
    4.  Additionally, while not a "missing disjointness" per se, it's good practice to connect central classes. `gao:AIModel` is clearly an `irao:InformaticsResearchArtifact`.
        ```turtle
        gao:AIModel rdfs:subClassOf irao:InformaticsResearchArtifact .
        ```

**P13: Inverse relationships not explicitly declared**
*   **Affected:** `irao:hasAuthor`, `gao:hasProvider`, `gao:hasLocation`, `gao:hasEnergyMetricsAllExperiments`, `gao:usedEnergyMeasurementServiceAllExperiments`
*   **Analysis:** For better querying and inferencing, object properties should often have their inverse declared.
*   **Proposed Axioms:** Define an inverse property for each. For completeness, I will also explicitly state the `rdfs:domain` and `rdfs:range` for the inverse properties.

    1.  For `irao:hasAuthor` (domain `irao:InformaticsResearchArtifact`, range `irao:Researcher`):
        ```turtle
        irao:isAuthorOf rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
            owl:inverseOf irao:hasAuthor ;
            rdfs:domain irao:Researcher ;
            rdfs:range irao:InformaticsResearchArtifact ;
            rdfs:label "is author of"@en .
        ```
    2.  For `gao:hasProvider` (domain `gao:HardwareSettings`, range `gao:Provider`):

        gao:isProviderFor rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;

    3.  For `gao:hasLocation`:

**P34: Untyped class**
*   **Affected:** `foaf:Person`, `vivo:Project`
*   **Analysis:** The provided ontology snippet *does* declare `foaf:Person` and `vivo:Project` as `rdf:type owl:Class`. However, OOPS! often flags classes as "untyped" if they lack sufficient descriptive axioms or are not well-integrated into the local ontology. Given that P08 already covers missing labels, for P34, adding `rdfs:comment` provides further definition and addresses potential "untyped" issues beyond just the `rdf:type` assertion.
*   **Proposed Axioms:** Add an `rdfs:comment` to each class.

    ```turtle
    foaf:Person rdfs:comment "A person, individual, or agent."@en .
    vivo:Project rdfs:comment "A planned piece of work or activity, often with a defined scope and outcome."@en .
    ```
