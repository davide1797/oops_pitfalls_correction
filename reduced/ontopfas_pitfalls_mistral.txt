=== PITFALLS DISCOVERY ===
### **Ontological Pitfalls & Resolutions**

#### **1. Inconsistent Property Domains/Ranges**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:affectedBy` and `ontopfas:affects` have mismatched domains/ranges:
  - `affectedBy` (domain: `schema:Place`, range: `obo:ExO_0000022`)
  - `affects` (domain: `obo:ExO_0000022`, range: `schema:Place`)
  - **Issue**: A PFAS (`obo:ExO_0000022`) cannot be both a **Place** and an **Exposure stimulus** (inverse relation).
- **Solution**: Align domains/ranges with **Pfas** (`ontopfas:Pfas`) instead of `ExO_0000022` (which is an exposure event, not a PFAS).
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:affectedBy rdfs:domain ontopfas:Pfas ;
                      rdfs:range schema:Place .
  ontopfas:affects rdfs:domain schema:Place ;
                   rdfs:range ontopfas:Pfas .
  ```

---

#### **2. Incorrect Inverse Property Definition**
- **Pitfall**: `obo:RO_0002221` ("surrounds") is marked as **symmetric** but has an inverse (`obo:RO_0002220`).
  - **Issue**: Symmetric properties should not have distinct inverses unless they are also **transitive** (which they are not).
- **Solution**: Remove `owl:symmetric` or clarify that it is **asymmetric** (since "surrounded by" ≠ "surrounds").
  ```turtle
  obo:RO_0002221 rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
                 owl:inverseOf obo:RO_0002220 ;
                 rdfs:label "surrounds" .
  ```

---

#### **3. Misaligned Class Hierarchy (Pfas vs. CHEBI)**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:Pfas` is a subclass of `obo:CHEBI_24431` ("Chemical entity"), but `obo:CHEBI_24531` ("Perfluoroalkyl") is also a subclass of `ontopfas:Pfas`.
  - **Issue**: Circular hierarchy (`Pfas` → `CHEBI_24431` → `CHEBI_24531` → `Pfas`).
- **Solution**: Either:
  - Make `ontopfas:Pfas` a **disjoint** subclass of `obo:CHEBI_24431` (if PFAS ≠ CHEBI chemicals), or
  - Align `ontopfas:Pfas` under a broader **PCO** (e.g., `obo:PCO_0000031` → `obo:CHEBI_24431`).
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:Pfas rdfs:subClassOf obo:PCO_0000031 .  # (if PFAS are organismal entities)
  ```

---

#### **4. Ambiguous Property Domains (e.g., `ontopfas:source`)**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:source` is defined as an **ObjectProperty** but lacks constraints.
  - **Issue**: Could link any class to any other, reducing clarity.
- **Solution**: Restrict domain to `ontopfas:Pfas` and range to `obo:CHEBI_24431` or `schema:Organization`.
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:source rdfs:domain ontopfas:Pfas ;
                   rdfs:range schema:Organization .
  ```

---

#### **5. Inconsistent Use of Datatype vs. Object Properties**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:isEther` and `ontopfas:isHydrophilic` are **DatatypeProperties** (range: `xsd:boolean`), but could be **ObjectProperties** linking to specific classes (e.g., `ontopfas:EtherPFAS`).
  - **Issue**: Mixing **Datatype** and **Object** properties for similar concepts.
- **Solution**: Either:
  - Enumerate possible values as classes (e.g., `ontopfas:EtherPFAS` as subclass of `ontopfas:Pfas`), or
  - Keep as **DatatypeProperty** but add **restrictions** (e.g., `owl:hasValue true`).
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:EtherPFAS rdfs:subClassOf ontopfas:Pfas ;
                      skos:definition "PFAS with ether functional group." .
  ```

---

#### **6. Undefined Property Restrictions (e.g., `ontopfas:LongChainPfas`)**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:LongChainPfas` is defined via **Restriction** but lacks formal axioms.
  - **Issue**: No clear way to enforce "PFCA with ≥8 carbons" or "PFSA with ≥6 carbons."
- **Solution**: Add **SWRL** or **OWL** restrictions:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:LongChainPfas owl:equivalentClass [
    a owl:Restriction ;
    owl:onProperty ontopfas:numberOfCarbons ;
    owl:minCardinality 8 ;
    owl:qualifiedClass ontopfas:Pfca
  ] ,
  [
    a owl:Restriction ;
    owl:onProperty ontopfas:numberOfCarbons ;
    owl:minCardinality 6 ;
    owl:qualifiedClass ontopfas:Pfsa
  ] .
  ```

---

#### **7. Missing Disjointness Between PFAS Subclasses**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:Pfca` and `ontopfas:Pfsa` are subclasses of `ontopfas:Pfaa` but may overlap (e.g., a PFAS with both carboxylic and sulfonic groups).
  - **Issue**: No **disjointness** axiom prevents incorrect classification.
- **Solution**: Add disjointness:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:Pfca owl:disjointWith ontopfas:Pfsa .
  ```

---

#### **8. Inconsistent Property Labels (e.g., `ontopfas:is`)**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:is` is defined as an **ObjectProperty** without domain/range or inverse.
  - **Issue**: Ambiguous purpose (could be a reification or identity relation).
- **Solution**: Replace with a **DatatypeProperty** (e.g., `owl:sameAs`) or define domain/range:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:is rdfs:subPropertyOf owl:sameAs .
  ```

---

#### **9. Misaligned `ontopfas:matrix` and `ontopfas:matrixOf`**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:matrix` links `sosa:Observation` → `ENVO_01000408` (Environmental zone), but `ontopfas:matrixOf` links `ENVO_01000408` → `sosa:Observation`.
  - **Issue**: `ENVO_01000408` is an **environmental zone**, not a **matrix** (which should be a **substance** or **material**).
- **Solution**: Use `obo:ENVO_0000000` (Environment) or `obo:CHEBI_24431` (Chemical entity) as range.
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:matrix rdfs:domain sosa:Observation ;
                   rdfs:range obo:CHEBI_24431 .
  ontopfas:matrixOf rdfs:domain obo:CHEBI_24431 ;
                    rdfs:range sosa:Observation .
  ```

---

#### **10. Ambiguous `ontopfas:validInPlace`**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:validInPlace` links `Regulation` → `schema:Place`, but its definition mentions "continent."
  - **Issue**: Should it be restricted to **Continent** (`schema:Continent`)?
- **Solution**: Add **domain restriction** or **SWRL rule**:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:validInPlace rdfs:domain ontopfas:Regulation ;
                       rdfs:range schema:Continent .
  ```

---
#### **11. Missing Property Chain Axioms**
- **Pitfall**: No **property chain** axioms for transitive relations like `ontopfas:containsMeasurement` → `ontopfas:measuredIn`.
  - **Issue**: Could lead to logical inconsistencies (e.g., `A containsMeasurement B` and `B measuredIn C` but no clear path).
- **Solution**: Add **property chain** (e.g., `ontopfas:containsMeasurement owl:propertyChainAxiom (ontopfas:measuredIn)`).
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:containsMeasurement owl:propertyChainAxiom (ontopfas:measuredIn) .
  ```

---
#### **12. Undefined `ontopfas:Paper` Hierarchy**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:Paper` is a subclass of `ontopfas:PhysicalObject`, but `ontopfas:PhysicalObject` is a subclass of `dcat:Dataset` (which is incorrect).
  - **Issue**: `Paper` should not be a subclass of `Dataset`.
- **Solution**: Align `ontopfas:Paper` under `schema:CreativeWork` or `schema:ScholarlyArticle`.
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:Paper rdfs:subClassOf schema:CreativeWork .
  ```

---
#### **13. Inconsistent `ontopfas:stimulatedBy` (Domain/Range)**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:stimulatedBy` links `ExO_0000022` → `ontopfas:Pfas`, but `ExO_0000022` is an **exposure event**, not a **PFAS**.
  - **Issue**: Should be `ontopfas:Pfas` → `ExO_0000022` (or vice versa with clearer semantics).
- **Solution**: Correct domain/range:
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:stimulatedBy rdfs:domain ontopfas:Pfas ;
                        rdfs:range obo:ExO_0000022 .
  ```

---
#### **14. Missing Functional Group Constraints**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:functionalGroup` is a **subPropertyOf part_of**, but no **cardinality** or **exactness** constraints exist.
  - **Issue**: A PFAS could have multiple functional groups (e.g., PFCA + PFSA).
- **Solution**: Add **maxCardinality 1** or **SWRL rules** to enforce exclusivity.
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:functionalGroup rdfs:subClassOf [
    a owl:Restriction ;
    owl:onProperty ontopfas:functionalGroup ;
    owl:maxCardinality 1
  ] .
  ```

---
#### **15. Inconsistent `ontopfas:sourceText` (Domain/Range)**
- **Pitfall**: `ontopfas:sourceText` links `schema:Organization` → `xsd:string`, but its definition is unclear.
  - **Issue**: Should it be a **DatatypeProperty** for metadata or an **ObjectProperty** linking to `schema:Text`?
- **Solution**: Replace with `schema:description` or clarify purpose.
  ```turtle
  ontopfas:sourceText rdfs:subPropertyOf schema:description .
  ```
