=== PITFALLS DISCOVERY ===
Here are key **ontological pitfalls** and **resolutive axioms** for the Green-AI Ontology:

---

### 1. **Inconsistent Property Domains**
   - **Pitfall**: Some properties (e.g., `hasIdentifier`, `hasName`, `hasURL`) lack explicit domain classes, while others (e.g., `hasCPU`, `hasEnergyMetrics`) are tied to specific classes. This may lead to ambiguity or misuse.
   - **Solution**: Define domains for all properties using `rdfs:domain`.
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasIdentifier> rdfs:domain <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/AIModel> .
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasName> rdfs:domain <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/AIModel> .
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasURL> rdfs:domain <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/AIModel> .
     ```

---

### 2. **Duplicate Property Labels**
   - **Pitfall**: `hasEnergyMetrics` and `hasEnergyMetricsAllExperiments` share the same label, risking confusion.
   - **Solution**: Distinguish labels (e.g., "per experiment" vs. "all experiments").
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasEnergyMetricsAllExperiments> rdfs:label "has energy metrics (all experiments)"@en .
     ```

---

### 3. **Inheritance Ambiguity**
   - **Pitfall**: `EnergyMeasurementService` and `EnergyMeasurementServiceAllExperiments` are subclasses of `EnergyMeasure`, but `EnergyMeasure` is a *datatype* (not an `owl:Class`). This creates a logical inconsistency.
   - **Solution**: Replace `EnergyMeasure` as a superclass with a more appropriate class (e.g., `EnergyMeasurement`).
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/EnergyMeasurementService> rdfs:subClassOf <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/EnergyMeasurement> .
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/EnergyMeasurementServiceAllExperiments> rdfs:subClassOf <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/EnergyMeasurement> .
     ```

---

### 4. **Missing Disjointness**
   - **Pitfall**: `CPU` and `GPU` are distinct but not explicitly declared as disjoint (e.g., a hardware setting cannot simultaneously have a CPU and GPU).
   - **Solution**: Define disjointness axioms.
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/CPU> owl:disjointWith <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/GPU> .
     ```

---

### 5. **Redundant Datatype Properties**
   - **Pitfall**: `hasEnergyConsumption` and `haskWh` (note typo in prefix) seem redundant for energy metrics. Similarly, `hasFPO` and `hasWatt`/`hasJoul` may overlap.
   - **Solution**: Align or merge properties (e.g., `hasEnergyConsumption` could be a superproperty of `haskWh`/`hasJoul`).
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasEnergyConsumption> rdfs:subPropertyOf <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/haskWh> .
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasWatt> rdfs:subPropertyOf <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasEnergyConsumption> .
     ```

---

### 6. **Missing Functional Properties**
   - **Pitfall**: Properties like `hasCPUCount` or `hasGPUCount` may allow multiple values (e.g., an AI model could have multiple conflicting CPU counts).
   - **Solution**: Declare them as `owl:FunctionalProperty`.
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasCPUCount> a owl:FunctionalProperty .
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasGPUCount> a owl:FunctionalProperty .
     ```

---

### 7. **Typo in Property Name**
   - **Pitfall**: `allExperminentsRunTime` has a typo ("Experminents" instead of "Experiments").
   - **Solution**: Correct the property name.
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/allExperimentsRunTime> rdfs:label "all experiments run time"@en .
     ```

---

### 8. **Unclear Relationships**
   - **Pitfall**: `HardwareSettings` and `SoftwareSettings` lack explicit constraints (e.g., a GPU must belong to hardware settings).
   - **Solution**: Add domain/range restrictions or `owl:hasKey` axioms.
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasGPU> rdfs:domain <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/HardwareSettings> .
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/hasGPU> rdfs:range <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/GPU> .
     ```

---

### 9. **Missing Inverse Properties**
   - **Pitfall**: `creates` (AIModel → InformaticsResearchArtifact) lacks an inverse, making bidirectional reasoning difficult.
   - **Solution**: Define inverse properties.
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/creates> owl:inverseOf <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/createdBy> .
     ```

---

### 10. **EnergyMeasure vs. EnergyMeasurementService Confusion**
   - **Pitfall**: `EnergyMeasure` is a class but also used as a range for `usedEnergyMeasurementService`, suggesting a mix of *measurement* and *service*.
   - **Solution**: Clarify hierarchy (e.g., `EnergyMeasurementService` *provides* `EnergyMeasure`).
   - **Axiom**:
     ```turtle
     <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/EnergyMeasurementService> owl:onProperty <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/providesEnergyMeasure> ;
       owl:someValuesFrom <https://w3id.org/Green-AI-Ontology/ontology/EnergyMeasure> .
     ```

---
**Summary**: Fix domain/range inconsistencies, disjointness, functional properties, typos, and clarify class hierarchies (e.g., `EnergyMeasure` vs. `EnergyMeasurementService`). Use `rdfs:subPropertyOf` and `owl:inverseOf` to resolve redundancies and improve reasoning.
